In this experiment, I benchmarked keyword search against embedding similarity to see which method better retrieves relevant context before prompting ChatGPT. The embedding model mapped passages and questions to normalized 384-dimensional vectors and ranked them using dot products. However, semantic similarity didn't always capture the full context: for Q1, embeddings retrieved the two-person rule passage but missed the access-hours information, even when raising k to 3 (whereas keyword search found both). For Q4, the system correctly noted that wavelength data was missing. Next, I plan to test a hybrid approach that merges keyword and embedding rankings to test whether it retrieves both Q1 passages at k = 2 without hurting performance on other questions.
